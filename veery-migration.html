Animal Migration

Animals migrate to find food, reproduce, and avoid harsh weather. Many birds travel south during winter to find warmer temperatures and more food. Climate change can affect migration by changing temperatures and weather patterns, which can cause birds to migrate earlier or later than usual. Heckscher (2018) explains how Veery migration and breeding patterns are connected to hurricane activity. In Colorado, I notice birds like geese migrating as the seasons change.

Veery Migration Map

Interpreting the Veery Migration Map

The map shows how Veeries move between North and South America throughout the year. During winter, most observations are in South America. In spring, Veeries move north to their breeding areas. During September and October, they begin moving south again. These patterns are consistent with Heckscher (2018).
