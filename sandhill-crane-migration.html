Sandhill Crane Migration

Research Question

How does the location of Sandhill Crane observations change throughout the year?

Methods

I used Sandhill Crane records from GBIF collected in 2024. I used Python and GeoPandas to organize the data and match observations to different ecoregions. I then calculated observation values and created an interactive map using hvPlot to compare observations by month.

Interactive Sandhill Crane Migration Map

Use the month selector to see how Sandhill Crane observations change throughout the year. The colors show observations in different ecoregions, not exact bird populations.

Results

The map shows seasonal changes in Sandhill Crane observations. During winter, observations are mostly found in southern parts of North America. During summer, observations go farther north into Canada. By fall, observations begin moving south again.

Interpretation

The results show that Sandhill Cranes move between northern and southern habitats throughout the year. They spend warmer months in northern breeding areas and move south when temperatures get colder. This matches what is known about seasonal migration of Sandhill Cranes. However, GBIF observations depend on where people report sightings, so the map does not show exact population sizes.
